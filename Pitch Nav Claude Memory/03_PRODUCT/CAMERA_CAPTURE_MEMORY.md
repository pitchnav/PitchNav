# Camera Capture Memory

## Previously preferred setup

- Side view
- Approximately 15 feet away
- Phone approximately 6 feet high
- Full body visible
- 240 fps preferred
- 120 fps acceptable

## Product-design requirements

- Professional scientific illustration
- Realistic mound or floor
- No floating athlete
- Simple wording
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Clear pass or retake result

## Technical questions

- Is 6 feet the lens height or phone mount height?
- Is 15 feet valid for every device lens?
- Does the full pitching motion need to remain in frame?
- Which side view is supported for left- and right-handed pitchers?
- What frame rates are actually processed?
- What resolution and lighting thresholds apply?
- What happens when landmark confidence is low?
