# Marketing System

## Core content pillars

1. Pitching education
2. Mechanics myths
3. Camera setup
4. Product demonstrations
5. Founder story
6. Progress with permission
7. Coach and facility value

## Short-form structure

1. Hook immediately
2. One problem
3. One useful insight
4. Product connection
5. Clear CTA

## Good marketing question

> What does the athlete understand or do differently after seeing this?

## Claims control

Do not invent:

- Velocity gains
- Injury reduction
- Accuracy percentages
- User counts
- Professional adoption
- Lab equivalence

## Draft-only agent rule

The Marketing Agent may create:

- Hooks
- Scripts
- Captions
- AI-video prompts
- Content calendars
- Ad variations

It may not publish or spend without approval.
