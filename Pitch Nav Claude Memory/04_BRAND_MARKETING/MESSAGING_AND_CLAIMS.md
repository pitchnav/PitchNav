# Messaging and Claims

## Safer language

- Analyze supported movement patterns from smartphone video.
- Identify a high-priority development focus.
- Track selected measurements over time.
- Improve the clarity of pitching feedback.
- Complement coaching.

## Requires validation

- Exact accuracy
- Laboratory equivalence
- Velocity improvement
- Injury-risk prediction
- Injury prevention
- Device-independent performance

## Prohibited without extraordinary evidence

- Guaranteed velocity
- Injury-proof
- Medical-grade diagnosis
- Perfect mechanics
- Works from any video
- Replaces a medical professional
