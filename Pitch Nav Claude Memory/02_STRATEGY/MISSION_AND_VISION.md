# Mission and Vision

## Mission

Make useful pitching analysis and development guidance accessible through smartphone video, while remaining honest about measurement limits and athlete safety.

## Vision

Pitch Nav becomes an ongoing development system that helps athletes and coaches move from:

**recording → measurement → understanding → priority → action → progress**

## Problem

Traditional biomechanics assessment can be expensive, inconvenient, and difficult to repeat. Video coaching can also be subjective or overwhelming.

## Opportunity

A smartphone product can make repeatable feedback available to more athletes, provided that it:

- Validates recording quality
- Shows measurement confidence
- Avoids unsupported medical claims
- Gives a clear next action
- Tracks progress over time

## Long-term identity

Pitch Nav should become more than a report generator. It should become a trusted pitching-development operating system for athletes, coaches, and facilities.
