# Positioning

## Core position

Advanced pitching intelligence made more accessible through a smartphone.

## Against expensive labs

Pitch Nav can emphasize:

- No special facility required
- Easier repeat assessment
- Lower access barrier
- Clear priorities and development tracking

Do not claim identical accuracy or identical capabilities to a laboratory unless validation proves it.

## Against generic AI

Pitch Nav should not feel like a chatbot guessing from video. It should feel like a structured measurement and development product with clear confidence and rules.

## Against ordinary video review

The advantage should be:

- Repeatable measurements
- Organized history
- Prioritized findings
- Progress tracking
- Consistent explanations
