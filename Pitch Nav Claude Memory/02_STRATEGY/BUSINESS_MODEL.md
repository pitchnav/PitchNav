# Business Model

## Potential customer groups

- Individual pitchers
- Parents
- Private pitching coaches
- Training facilities
- Teams and organizations

## Potential offers

### Individual

- Analysis
- Progress history
- Development priorities
- Premium reports

### Coach

- Multiple athletes
- Coach notes
- Shared reports
- Athlete organization

### Facility or team

- Staff roles
- Organization dashboard
- Usage reporting
- Volume access
- Onboarding support

## Unknowns

- Final pricing
- Trial structure
- Processing cost per analysis
- Storage cost
- Retention behavior
- Coach willingness to pay
