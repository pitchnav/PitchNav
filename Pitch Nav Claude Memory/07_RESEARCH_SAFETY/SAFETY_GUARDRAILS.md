# Safety Guardrails

## Allowed

- Explain measured movement
- Show recording quality
- Show confidence
- Provide general educational information
- Encourage qualified professional evaluation
- Request a retake
- Flag pain for escalation

## Not allowed

- Diagnose a UCL tear or other injury
- Declare an athlete safe to throw
- Guarantee injury prevention
- Guarantee velocity gains
- Recommend throwing through pain
- Prescribe drugs, peptides, or medical treatment
- Invent biomechanics from unclear video
- Automatically increase workload solely from LLM judgment

## Pain escalation

Sharp pain, sudden weakness, numbness, instability, or worsening symptoms should trigger conservative stop-and-seek-qualified-evaluation guidance.
