# Evidence Grading

## A — Strong

Multiple relevant, high-quality studies with reliable methods and consistent results.

## B — Moderate

Useful evidence with meaningful limitations.

## C — Preliminary

Small, indirect, correlational, or not yet replicated.

## D — Weak

Anecdote, unclear method, marketing claim, or unsupported opinion.

## Every research conclusion needs

- Source
- Population
- Method
- Result
- Limitation
- Grade
- Direct evidence
- Product inference
- Validation needed
