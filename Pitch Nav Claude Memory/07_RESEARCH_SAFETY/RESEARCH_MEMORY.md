# Research Memory

## Priority questions

- Which measurements are reliable from one side-view phone video?
- How does frame rate affect timing metrics?
- What camera requirements are necessary?
- How repeatable are measurements across sessions and devices?
- Which movement observations are actionable?
- Which drill recommendations are evidence-supported?
- What product claims can be supported?

## Evidence process

1. Define the product question.
2. Prefer primary research.
3. Record population and method.
4. Record measurement technology.
5. Record results and limitations.
6. Grade evidence.
7. Separate direct evidence from product inference.
8. Define internal validation.
