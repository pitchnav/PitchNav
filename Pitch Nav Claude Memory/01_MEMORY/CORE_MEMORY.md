# Core Memory

## Company

- **Name:** Pitch Nav
- **Founder:** Luke
- **Category:** Baseball pitching analysis and player development
- **Primary concept:** Use smartphone video to make useful pitching biomechanics and development guidance more accessible.
- **Long-term direction:** Move from a one-time analysis product toward an ongoing pitching-development operating system.

## Product vision

Pitch Nav should help a pitcher or coach:

1. Record a supported delivery video.
2. Confirm that the recording is usable.
3. Understand measurable movement patterns.
4. Identify the highest-priority development opportunity.
5. Receive a practical next step.
6. Track changes over time.

## Positioning

Pitch Nav is intended to reduce dependence on expensive in-person biomechanics laboratories while staying honest about the limits of smartphone analysis.

It should feel:

- Premium
- Scientific
- Professional
- Modern
- Athlete-centered
- Easy to understand

## Brand direction

- Dark navy, blue, and white
- Realistic and scientific visuals
- Avoid cartoon-like anatomy or floating, unrealistic scenes
- Smooth modern interface
- Strong mobile experience
- Simple user-facing wording
- Existing direction includes:
  - “Navigate Your Next Level”
  - “Analyze. Adjust. Advance.”
  - “Understand Your Delivery. Build Better Velocity.” as a draft marketing direction, not a guaranteed outcome

## Recording direction currently remembered

Draft camera guidance previously discussed:

- Side view
- Camera approximately 15 feet away
- Phone approximately 6 feet high
- Full body visible
- 240 fps preferred
- 120 fps acceptable

These settings must be validated against the actual product pipeline before being treated as final technical requirements.
